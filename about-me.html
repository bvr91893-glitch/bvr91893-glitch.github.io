About Bryan V. Roache

Bryan V. Roache is an accessibility and equity consultant who designs and consults with accessibility at the center.

Quick Intro

Bryan V. Roache, spelled R-O-A-C-H-E, is a male student who is blind and has cerebral palsy. He also uses a medical walking boot on his right leg.

His portfolio focuses on accessibility-first web design, practical technology help, and real-world consulting for people who need tools to work better.

  • Accessibility is lived experience, not an extra feature.
  • Web pages should be clear, useful, and practical for real people.
  • Assistive technology and everyday technology both belong in the design conversation.

What This Page Shows

Accessibility point of view

Accessibility Is Not Extra. It Is Lived Experience.

Accessibility is not just a feature; it is part of Bryan's lived experience. His life and education have taught him that access should be part of the foundation, not something added later.

Design point of view

Practicality Over Pretty

When Bryan designs web pages, he keeps accessibility in mind. His motto is "practicality over pretty," because a website should not only look good; it should work well for real people.

Bryan believes accessible design should be clear, dependable, and usable with assistive technology. A page should make sense to someone using a screen reader, keyboard, magnification, or any practical access tool that helps them participate fully.
