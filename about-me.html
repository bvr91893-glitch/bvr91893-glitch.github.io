About Brian V. Roache

Brian V. Roache is an accessibility and equity consultant who designs and consults with accessibility at the center.

Personal story Accessibility Is Not Extra. It Is Lived Experience.

Brian V. Roache, spelled R-O-A-C-H-E, is a male student who is blind and has cerebral palsy. He also uses a medical walking boot on his right leg.

Accessibility is not just a feature; it is part of Brian's lived experience. His life and education have taught him that access should be part of the foundation, not something added later.

Design point of view Practicality Over Pretty

When Brian designs web pages, he keeps accessibility in mind. His motto is "practicality over pretty," because a website should not only look good; it should work well for real people.

Brian believes accessible design should be clear, dependable, and usable with assistive technology. A page should make sense to someone using a screen reader, keyboard, magnification, or any practical access tool that helps them participate fully.
