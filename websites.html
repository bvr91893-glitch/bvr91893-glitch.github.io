My ITE 170 Portfolio

The site you're reading right now. A multi-page personal portfolio I built from scratch as my ITE 170 capstone at NOVA Annandale.

What's on it: a hero slideshow with a pause control, a clickable image gallery that opens a keyboard-navigable lightbox, a featured video, an about page, project sub-pages (certifications, AI projects, websites), and a full-screen contact form wired up to Formspree.

What I focused on: full mobile responsiveness with a hamburger nav, and accessibility from the ground up — skip links, ARIA landmarks, real form labels, focus management, and prefers-reduced-motion support so it's usable with a screen reader.
